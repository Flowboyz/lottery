How To Play

🎲 The Game

Ditto Dinky is a pick-3 lottery game. Choose 3 numbers (1-5), place your bet, and match the Lucky Number to win 5x your bet!

🔢 Lucky Number System

Your 3 picked numbers are added together to get your total (e.g., 2+3+4 = 9). A Lucky Number between 3-15 is generated. If the Lucky Number matches your total, you win!

💰 Payout System

Win: Your bet amount × 5. Bet ₦1,000, win ₦5,000!

Loss: Your bet amount is deducted from your balance.

Win probability: 10% per play.
