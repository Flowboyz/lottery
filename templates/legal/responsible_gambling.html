Responsible Gambling

Our Commitment

Ditto Dinky is committed to promoting responsible gambling. We provide tools and resources to help you stay in control of your gaming activity.

Daily Betting Limits

A daily betting limit is automatically enforced on all accounts. This limit resets at midnight each day. You cannot place bets exceeding this limit within a 24-hour period.

Self-Exclusion

If you feel you need a break from gaming, you can activate self-exclusion from your account. During self-exclusion, you will be unable to place any bets. You may still deposit and withdraw funds.

Signs of Problem Gambling

If you recognize any of these signs, consider taking a break or seeking help: spending more than you can afford, chasing losses, neglecting responsibilities due to gambling, borrowing money to gamble, or feeling anxious or distressed about your gambling.

Getting Help

If you or someone you know has a gambling problem, please reach out to a qualified professional or contact a gambling helpline in your region.

Age Restriction

You must be at least 18 years old to use this platform. We reserve the right to request age verification at any time.
