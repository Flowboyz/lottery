🎴 How to Play Whot

Whot is a classic card game where players take turns matching the shape or number of the active card on the discard pile.

⚔️
Stake & Match

Agree on a stake from ₦500 to ₦5000. First player to play all cards wins the entire pool (minus commission).

⚡
Match Card

Play a card that matches either the Shape (Circle, Triangle, Cross, Star, Square) or the Number of the top card.

2️⃣
Pick Two (2) - Stackable!

Playing a 2 forces your opponent to draw 2 cards. However, if your opponent also has a 2, they can play it to stack and pass the penalty, forcing you to draw 4 (unless stacked further!).

3️⃣
Pick Three (Card 5) - Stackable!

Playing a **5 (Pick Three)** forces your opponent to draw 3 cards. If they also have a 5, they can stack it to pass a 6-card penalty back to you!

⏱️
Hold On / Suspend (1 & 8)

Playing a 1 (Hold On) or an 8 (Suspend) stops your opponent's turn, letting you play another card immediately.

🛒
General Market (14)

Playing a 14 forces your opponent to draw 1 card from the deck immediately.

🃏
Whot Wildcard (20)

Playing a 20 acts as a wildcard. You can play it on any card and call a new shape that the next player must match.

🛑
15-Second Turn Timeout

Each player has 15 seconds to make a move. If the timer runs out, the server automatically draws a card for you and switches the turn.
